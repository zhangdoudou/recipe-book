# Shandong-Style Stir-Fried Chicken

## Ingredients

- Chicken pieces
- Green peppers
- Ginger and garlic
- Dried chili peppers
- Star anise
- Soy sauce
- Cooking wine
- Salt and sugar
- Cooking oil

## Instructions

- Stir-fry the chicken in hot oil until lightly browned.
- Add ginger, garlic, and dried chilies; cook until fragrant.
- Add soy sauce, cooking wine, salt, and a little sugar.
- Add green peppers and stir-fry until the chicken is fully cooked.
- Serve hot.
